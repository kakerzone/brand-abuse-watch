# Correlation graph

An edge means only that two or more domains share an observed indicator. It does not prove common ownership or malicious coordination. Preserve the indicator type/value and source evidence so a reviewer can verify it.

High-value indicators include an exact IP, TLS/certificate fingerprint, certificate issuer plus serial, nameserver, registrar, favicon hash, or content hash. Do not group assets by generic certificate issuer, generic page title, or brand keyword alone. Use graph edges to prioritize review and discover adjacent assets, never as a standalone enforcement decision.
