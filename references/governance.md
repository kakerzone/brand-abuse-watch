# Internal governance

Separate analyst, reviewer, and administrator roles. Record profile changes, source collection, score overrides, allowlist decisions, and case disposition with timestamp and actor. Retain raw evidence according to company policy; protect screenshots, URLs, and partner data as security-sensitive artifacts.

Promotion readiness requires source health monitoring, rate-limit handling, secret storage outside repository files, documented coverage gaps, a false-positive feedback loop, and fixtures for known benign, known malicious, and unresolved cases. External reporting, blocking, and takedown remain human-approved actions.
